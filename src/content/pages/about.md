Hey, I’m Suraj.

This is my little corner of the internet: a place to share my thoughts, document what I’m learning, and write about the things I build.

I want this space to be a record of the process. The questions as well as the answers. The experiments as well as the things that work.

## Learning in public

I’m planning [#100DaysOfLearningAI](/series/100-days-of-learning-ai/): one new thing about artificial intelligence, followed by a note on what I understood, what I tried, and what I’m still curious about.

Each day will have its own post. The series page will keep them in order, so you can start at the beginning or dip into a topic that interests you.
